# Third-party software and attribution

This project builds on
[IncompressibleNavierStokes.jl](https://github.com/agdestein/IncompressibleNavierStokes.jl),
an energy-conserving incompressible Navier-Stokes solver developed by Syver
Doving Agdestein, Benjamin Sanderse, and contributors.

The full-order solver, its staggered-grid discretization, pressure projection,
time integration, and GPU infrastructure are upstream work. The ROM workflows,
case-study orchestration, diagnostics, experiments, analysis, and thesis in
this repository were developed by Francisco Abajo Lis.

The upstream MIT license is reproduced in `LICENSE-UPSTREAM`.
